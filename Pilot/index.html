A community idea in testing

One local service.
More ways to book.

A business could offer an individual booking and set better prices as more nearby households join its group offer. Friends and neighbours share the opportunity; the business chooses the prices, dates and group sizes.

The idea at a glance

Businesses create the offer. Neighbours choose how to join.

The business sets the service area, dates, capacity, individual price if offered, and each group discount stage.

01 / Set the terms

🏪

A business publishes an offer

It decides what service it can provide and which group sizes make a better price possible.

02 / Choose a path

👋

Households join or book alone

Customers can join the group and invite friends, or choose the stated individual price if that option is offered.

03 / Unlock a stage

🔓

The group price improves

When a stated booking threshold is reached, that stage's discount is locked in under the offer's terms.
