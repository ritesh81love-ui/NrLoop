Business Terms & Conditions

One last thing — this is what keeps prices, deals and protections trustworthy for the customers who join your groups.

Keep bookings on NrLoop. Once a customer joins your deal through NrLoop, please don't invite them to book, pay, or rebook with you directly outside the platform for that same service. This is what keeps their price-lock, guarantee and refund protection valid — if a booking moves off-platform, NrLoop can no longer protect that customer if something goes wrong.

Accurate listings. Deals must reflect a genuine service you can actually deliver at the price and group size stated.

Fair dealing with repeat customers. Customers who complete a deal with you are welcome to book with you again — just keep future bookings through NrLoop too, so their loyalty perks and your repeat-customer credit keep counting.

Platform fee. The platform fee is deducted automatically from each completed booking, shown clearly on your dashboard before you launch a deal.
