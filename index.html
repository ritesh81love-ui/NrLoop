Hey neighbour —
this is early, and your take on it matters.

This is a simple idea being tested: nearby households joining up on the services they already need — pest control, cleaning, gardening, handyman work and more — so the price drops once enough neighbours join in, and local businesses get a fuller, more efficient day instead of scattered one-off jobs. If you need something done quickly and can't wait for a group to form, you can still book the same trusted providers individually — group deals are the savings path, not the only path. What matters most right now is one thing: would this actually get used, and what would make it better?

💰
Running a household adds up faster than most people track.

Between pest control, house cleaning, gardening, carpet cleaning, electrical and gas repairs, and aircon and heating servicing, Australian cost guides commonly put an actively-maintained home somewhere around $3,000–$5,000 a year — often more, depending on property size and location. Grouping bookings together is one practical way for neighbours to push back against rising costs. This is just Stage 1 — Stage 2 is planned to bring in more categories that stretch the household budget even further, so there's good reason to get in early while this is still taking shape.
